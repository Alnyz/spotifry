The equalizer in app/src/main/java/com/spotifry/music/player/Equalizer.kt adapts the
peak filter, frequency response and fade between settings from Mochy Music
(https://github.com/realidkroo/Mochy-Music, files eq/audio/BiquadFilter.kt and
eq/audio/CustomEqualizerAudioProcessor.kt).
