Spotifry's equalizer page and filters are adapted from Mochy Music
(https://github.com/realidkroo/Mochy-Music):

- app/src/main/java/com/spotifry/music/player/Equalizer.kt adapts the peak filter,
  frequency response and fade between settings from eq/audio/BiquadFilter.kt and
  eq/audio/CustomEqualizerAudioProcessor.kt.
- app/src/main/java/com/spotifry/music/ui/settings/EqualizerScreen.kt adapts the page,
  graph and sweep-to-draw bands from ui/screens/equalizer/EqualizerScreen.kt,
  ui/screens/equalizer/EqGraphEditor.kt and ui/screens/equalizer/EqBandsEditor.kt.
